# Google Drive

Google Drive as Storage locations: My Drive, one of its folders, or a shared drive, live. Add as many as you want, each with its own Google account.

## Set up

1. In Files, choose **Add location**, then Google Drive.
2. Enter a folder's address or ID, or leave it empty for all of My Drive.
3. Sign in with Google in the browser.

You can sign in with your own Google app: create an OAuth client in Google Cloud, enable the Drive API, and enter its client ID and secret.

## Good to know

- Google's own documents are read in Office formats.
- Deleting puts a file in Drive's trash.
