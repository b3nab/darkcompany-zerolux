# Projects

Projects and their tasks, for people and agents. Keep them in ZeroLux, or see them where they already are: a tracker's workspace shows live, with nothing copied.

## What you can do

- Plan projects and tasks on a board, by status, priority and due date.
- Give a task to an agent: its sessions are told of it, and its work stays with the task.
- Add as many trackers as you want, several of one too.

## Sources

**ZeroLux** keeps projects in the workspace and is there from the start. Every other source is a tracker's workspace, added with **Add source** in Projects. Linear, Plane and ClickUp come with their own extensions.

Removing a source forgets it in ZeroLux; nothing changes in its tracker.
