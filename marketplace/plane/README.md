# Plane

Plane workspaces as Projects sources, in Plane's cloud or one you host: their projects and work items, live.

## Set up

1. In Plane, create a personal access token in your profile settings.
2. In Projects, choose **Add source**, then Plane.
3. Enter the workspace's slug, as in its address, and the token. For a Plane you host, enter its address too.

Work items keep their state, priority, labels, due date and comments.
