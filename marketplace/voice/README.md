# Voice

Speech to text in chats and the Console, and a voice of its own for each agent.

## Speech to text

Recordings become text on this computer with the engine you choose in Settings: whisper.cpp (Metal, CUDA, Vulkan or the processor), faster-whisper or Parakeet v3. Download, switch and delete models there, and choose how long the model stays loaded. Whisper is given the names of the workspace, its people and its agents.

## Agents' voices

1. **Design**: describe an agent's voice, and choose among three.
2. **Speak**: the agent speaks with it, in English or Italian.

## Good to know

Everything runs on the computer ZeroLux runs on: no audio leaves it. Transcription models download from Settings; the first one by itself. Voices are spoken with Kyutai's Pocket TTS, whose weights are CC BY 4.0.
