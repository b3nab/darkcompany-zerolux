# Voice

Speech to text in chats and the Console, and a voice of its own for each agent.

## Speech to text

Recordings become text with the model the workspace chooses in Settings. Whisper is given the names of the workspace, its people and its agents.

## Agents' voices

1. **Design**: describe an agent's voice, and choose among three.
2. **Speak**: the agent speaks with it, in English or Italian.

## Good to know

Everything runs on the computer ZeroLux runs on: no audio leaves it. The models download the first time they are used. Voices are spoken with Kyutai's Pocket TTS, whose weights are CC BY 4.0.
